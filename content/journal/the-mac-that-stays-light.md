A cloud drive should feel like something you plugged in. Not like a tab you forgot, and not like a disk you dread opening.

Cove is your own cloud drive. One click, and it shows up on your Mac under Locations, beside Macintosh HD. The library lives in the cloud. The laptop stays itself.

## The quiet problem

Family film, a spring campaign, a studio session, a repo an agent is writing: they share one small system disk until something has to go. External drives fill drawers. Review links expire on Friday. The work got heavy. The laptop stayed the same size.

## What changes

Cove does not ask you to learn a new pile of folders in a browser. It shows up like a drive. Photos, Premiere, Logic, Capture One, Cursor, and the agents you already run open what they need. What you do not open does not need to fill the Mac.

## Who it is for

The product stays the same. The words change with the desk: home, marketing, studio, agents. Quiet confidence. Loud libraries.
