**YORKSHIRE, UK — 6 October 2026** — NOAM Co. today opened the waitlist for Cove, a private-beta Mac product that mounts a personal cloud drive beside Macintosh HD in Finder. One click, and the drive shows up. The files stay in the cloud. The Mac stays light.

Cove is built for desks that outgrew a small system disk: family film at home, campaigns in marketing, sessions in the studio, and repos shared with AI agents. It is not another browser folder, and it is not a review link that expires on Friday.

The Mac app is not a public download. Seats open from the waitlist. An invite ladder lets people climb by referring others. iPhone access is planned for a later release. Mt. Mtn. is the organisation-sized range of the same mount idea.

## From the studio

> We wanted the feeling of plugging in a drive, without filling the laptop again. Cove is that drive: yours, in the cloud, on your Mac.
