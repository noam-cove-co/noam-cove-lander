# Cove ICP: audience of one

Use these **named one-person scenes** when drafting LinkedIn, X, landers, or ads. Prefer one named person per piece over abstract segments.

Aligned with site desks in `src/config/site.ts` (`home`, `marketing`, `studio`, `agents`) plus commercial layers (agency, company, AI builders).

**Not for Story Bank.** Story Bank holds *your* receipts and off-limits. This file holds *who Cove is for*. Point writing skills here for audience colour.

---

## How to use

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Audience of one** | Speak to this person as if the post is only for them |
| **Scene** | What is full / broken on their Mac right now |
| **Jobs** | What Cove does for them in one line |
| **Channels** | Where they are most likely to hear us first |
| **Land** | Best first URL |

Do not invent client names. Desk labels and public product truths only.

## Personal media & family

### Wedding-film parent
- **Scene:** iPhone and MacBook both full after a wedding weekend; 4K clips, AirDrop failures, “which drive has the ceremony?”
- **Jobs:** Keep the masters in the cloud; open them on the Mac like a drive; share the same drive with a spouse without a dying link
- **Channels:** LinkedIn (soft), X, Meta later, `/land/out-of-space`
- **Land:** `/land/out-of-space` → `/land/join-the-list`

### Concert-and-holiday documentarian
- **Scene:** High-quality memories: festivals, holidays, school plays; phone keeps filling; laptop is the editing desk that cannot hold the library
- **Jobs:** Years of family film without a drawer of SSDs; brother opens Christmas without a Dropbox hunt
- **Channels:** X, Meta, organic LI stories
- **Land:** `/land/stays-light` or `/land/own-drive`

### Home film lead (family archive keeper)
- **Scene:** The person who “keeps the photos”; 2 TB+ of life across years; hates another external drive
- **Jobs:** One Family volume in Finder; stays light on every Mac in the house
- **Channels:** Word of mouth, LI personal posts, waitlist invites
- **Land:** `/land/join-the-list`

## Marketers & marketing teams

### Marketing lead on a MacBook with a 2 TB campaign
- **Scene:** Decks, selects, banner v17, Premiere project; team scattered; laptop choking on masters
- **Jobs:** One Spring Campaign drive every Mac can mount; producer on a train, editor in studio, same folders
- **Channels:** **LinkedIn primary**, X secondary
- **Land:** `/land/own-drive` or `/land/stays-light` → waitlist

### In-house content producer
- **Scene:** Always exporting; always out of space mid-cut; lives in Premiere / Final Cut / After Effects
- **Jobs:** Heavy files stay in the cloud; apps open the work as if a drive were plugged in
- **Channels:** LinkedIn, X
- **Land:** `/land/out-of-space`

### Performance marketer (Meta / paid social)
- **Scene:** Creative volume rising; disk full of UGC and statics; needs a clean library, not another SaaS link farm
- **Jobs:** Campaign creatives and masters on a real mount; desk language matches Marketing first seats
- **Channels:** LinkedIn thought leadership, later paid Meta
- **Land:** `/land/join-the-list`

## Agencies & companies

### Agency creative director
- **Scene:** Multiple clients’ assets mixed across drives and “final_final” folders; staff MacBooks cannot hold the library
- **Jobs:** Client work on dedicated volumes; staff mount the same drive; less courier-SSD theatre
- **Channels:** LinkedIn
- **Land:** `/land/own-drive` (Mt. Mtn. later if org-scale)

### Agency producer / traffic
- **Scene:** Version control is Slack + WeTransfer; deadline is Friday; masters must not live on one person’s laptop
- **Jobs:** Shared campaign drive; masters that do not expire Friday
- **Channels:** LinkedIn
- **Land:** `/land/join-the-list`

### Company brand / ops lead
- **Scene:** Brand kit, product film, event archives for a floor or whole company; compliance wants one place
- **Jobs:** Organisation-scale path (Cove → Mt. Mtn. when they outgrow personal/beta)
- **Channels:** LinkedIn, direct
- **Land:** `/mt` enquiry or waitlist with role = marketing

## AI agents, vibe coders, builders

### Vibe coder with a repo that outgrew the laptop
- **Scene:** Cursor / Claude / copilots writing into a project; `node_modules`, assets, transcripts; disk warnings mid-flow
- **Jobs:** Repo lives on a mounted cloud drive; agent and human share a real path
- **Channels:** **X + LinkedIn**, agent Twitter/X discourse
- **Land:** `/land/stays-light` → waitlist (role: agents)

### Agent operator (runs fleets / long sessions)
- **Scene:** Transcripts, tool outputs, multiple worktrees; system disk is the bottleneck, not the model
- **Jobs:** Agent desks: repos and transcripts off the laptop; still one click in Finder
- **Channels:** X, LinkedIn
- **Land:** `/` agents desk story + waitlist

### Indie hacker / solo builder
- **Scene:** Ships on a MacBook Air; media + code + notes; refuses to buy a 2 TB upgrade just to keep building
- **Jobs:** Own cloud drive beside Macintosh HD; keep shipping without the upgrade tax
- **Channels:** X first, LinkedIn second
- **Land:** `/land/join-the-list`

## Anti-patterns

- “Everyone who owns a Mac” (too wide; pick one person)
- Mixing Mt. Mtn. enterprise pitch into founding-beta consumer posts without a clear cut
- Naming real clients or reverse-identifiable details (see Story Bank off-limits)
- Inventing storage sizes that contradict campaign lock (**11GB** Look familiar; desks use their own cloudSize copy in `site.ts`)
