# Organic discovery · LinkedIn + X

Goal: get Cove in front of non-followers and turn that into waitlist leads.  
Hashtags alone will not do that in 2026.

## Short answer on hashtags

| Channel | Still useful? | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **LinkedIn** | Barely | Hashtag feeds / follow are gone. Tags are weak topic labels. Prefer **keywords in the post body**. 0–2 niche tags at the end is fine; 5+ is noise. |
| **X** | Slightly | 0–1 niche tag max. Broad tags (`#marketing`) are crowded. Keywords in the sentence beat a pile of `#`. |
| **Instagram** | Modest | Keep 3–6 niche tags (already in week-1 IG posts). Not the LinkedIn/X lever. |

Do not treat hashtags as the growth plan.

## Levers that still move organic → leads

### 1. Keywords in the first lines (both)
The algorithms and search read natural language. Put the searchable phrases in the caption, not behind a `#`.

**Cove discovery phrases** (pick 1–2 per post, from the ICP scene):
- Mac cloud drive / cloud drive on Mac
- Finder / Locations / Macintosh HD
- MacBook storage / out of space / 256 GB
- Premiere / Final Cut / Capture One (when the persona uses them)
- marketing team Mac / campaign masters
- private beta Mac

### 2. First-comment (or first-reply) link
External links in the **main body** suppress distribution on LinkedIn and especially X.

| Channel | Body | Where the waitlist URL goes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| LinkedIn | No link (or soft “link in first comment”) | **First comment** immediately after post |
| X | No link in the post | **First reply** |
| Instagram | Link in bio + optional caption URL | Bio primary |

UTMs stay on the first-comment / first-reply URL (`utm_source`, `utm_medium=social`, campaign, content).

### 3. Comment bait (LinkedIn especially)
Reach expands on quality replies in the first hour. End with **one real question** the ICP can answer in one line. Not “Thoughts?” Not engagement bait (“Like if you agree”).

Examples that fit Cove voice:
- What would you move off the laptop first if Finder had a second drive?
- How many GB free is your MacBook right now?
- Dropbox folder, external SSD, or “it lives on the producer’s machine”?

### 4. Native media + company vs personal
- Always attach the V2 creative (image beats text-only).
- On LinkedIn, **personal/founder posts** still out-distribute company-page posts for cold discovery. Company page for brand; personal for reach. Cross-post carefully; do not spam both with identical walls of text.
- Early engagement: reply to every real comment in the first 60 minutes.

### 5. Soft CTA, hard CTA in comment
Body sells the problem and the product truth. Waitlist ask lives in the first comment so the feed post can travel.

### 6. Optional tags (only if they earn their place)

**LinkedIn (0–2):** `#Mac` `#CloudStorage` or niche `#PrivateBeta` · skip `#marketing` `#AI` piles.

**X (0–1):** `#Mac` or `#BuildInPublic` when the post is founder-shaped · otherwise none.

Branded `#Cove` is optional branding, not a discovery engine until the brand has search volume.

## What not to do
- Hashtag walls
- Tagging strangers for reach
- Lead with the URL in the LinkedIn/X body
- Fake metrics or invented customer names
- Posting only from the company page and expecting cold reach
