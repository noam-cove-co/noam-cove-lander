# Cove social + B2B voice

## In one line
Quiet confidence. A drive that shows up. The Mac stays light.

**Claims law:** [`docs/growth/approved-claims.md`](../growth/approved-claims.md) (approved).  
**Personas:** [`docs/growth/icp.md`](../growth/icp.md). Hit one audience-of-one hard per post.

## Do
- British English; **UK first**
- Short sentences. One idea per post
- Name the product **Cove** and the maker **NOAM Co.** when craft matters
- Point to a real land URL with UTMs
- Match tone to asset: scrappy scribble vs broadsheet quiet
- Scarcity: exciting, deliberately vague (seats from the list; no counts or dates)
- App name-drops OK (Premiere, Logic, Capture One, Cursor, …) when the persona uses them

## Don’t
- Em dashes (use `:` or `|`)
- Hype (“revolutionary”, “game-changing”, “AI-powered cloud”)
- Fake metrics, fake logos, invented customers
- Seat counts, waitlist size, pricing, ship dates
- Promise public download
- Over-hashtag

## Channel lengths
| Channel | Caption budget |
| --- | --- |
| LinkedIn | 80–180 words, or a tight 40-word punch |
| Instagram | 40–120 words + optional 3–6 tags |
| X | ≤ 220 characters preferred |
| Press lead | ≤ 40 words for the nut graf |
| Blog intro | ≤ 60 words before the first subhead |
